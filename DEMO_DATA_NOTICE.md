# Demo data notice

Every organisation, person, site, carrier, circuit, document, finding, decision and
figure in this repository is **synthetic**. Together they describe a sample engagement
invented to show how the product works.

## The rules that hold this in place

1. **The client is fictional.** Acme Corp is an invented company. Nothing in the dataset
   refers to a real Stonemark client, prospect, or any company the owner has worked with.
2. **Carriers are fictional.** Carrier, access-provider, colocation and facility names are
   invented, and each is checked against the carrier registry and its historical brands
   before use. **No real carrier is ever named in a finding.**
3. **No real facility is named.** Site locations may use real metropolitan areas, but no
   real building, serving office, POP or CLLI code is named.
4. **Figures are sample declared inputs.** Costs shown as declared by the client are
   invented sample ranges, labelled as declared. Nothing is presented as a benchmark
   unless it exists in the claims register, and where a figure cannot be computed the
   demo says so rather than showing zero.
5. **No prior-employer material.** Nothing in this dataset derives from any current or
   former employer's customer data, pricing, records or internal documents.
6. **The visitor's browser holds everything.** The demo makes no database connection,
   collects no account, and stores only what the visitor's own browser keeps for their
   session. Reset returns it to the seed.

## What the demo is not

It is not an assessment, attestation, certification, audit or opinion about any real
network. No one may rely on it as one. See `DEMO_TERMS.md`.

## When real data is offered

If a prospect or client offers real data to make the demo more convincing, the answer is
no. Real client infrastructure data is confidential, belongs in the platform under an
engagement with a signed agreement, and never in this repository.
