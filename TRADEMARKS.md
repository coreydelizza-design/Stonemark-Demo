# Trademarks

## Stonemark's marks

| Mark | Form | Status |
|---|---|---|
| STONEMARK | Word mark | Intent-to-use filing planned, Classes 9 and 42. The descriptive suffix is excluded from the filing. |
| The mason's mark (the three-stroke `///`) | Design mark | Unregistered; used as a trademark and as the product's brand mark |
| Letter of Assessment | Used as a product name | [PENDING: counsel view on whether this is protectable or descriptive] |

Until a registration issues, use `™` rather than `®`. Never print `®` for an
unregistered mark: doing so is itself a misrepresentation in the United States.

## Use inside this repo

- The wordmark is set in all capitals with letter-spacing, weight 600, beside the mark.
- The mark is orange `#ef6b2e`. The evidence glyph on a site card is drawn in ink, never
  orange, so a data glyph can never be mistaken for the logo.
- Do not restyle, recolour, rotate, outline, animate or crop the mark, and do not build a
  new lockup from it.

## Use by anyone else

No right to use Stonemark's marks is granted by access to this repo. A contractor,
partner or client may use them only as a signed agreement allows.

## Third-party marks

- **Acme Corp** and every carrier, access provider, colocation operator and facility in
  the demo are **fictional**, invented for the demo, and are not intended to refer to any
  real organisation. Before a name enters the dataset it is checked against the carrier
  registry and its historical brands, per the build pack.
- No real carrier is ever named in a demo finding. A sales demo showing a named real
  carrier failing diversity is a claims exposure even when the data is labelled
  synthetic.
- Where a real product must be named (a browser, a cloud, a font), the name belongs to
  its owner and is used only descriptively.
- If any invented name turns out to match a real company in the same sector, treat it as
  a defect and rename it.
