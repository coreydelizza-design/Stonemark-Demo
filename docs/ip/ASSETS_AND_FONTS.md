# Assets and fonts

## Fonts

Inter, Archivo and Source Serif 4 are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The
OFL is permissive, including for commercial use, and it carries two obligations that
matter here:

1. The licence and copyright notice travel with the font files. Self-host the fonts in
   the repo, with the OFL text alongside, and reproduce the notices in
   `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
2. A modified font may not keep a reserved font name. Do not rename, subset-and-rename,
   or re-release a modified font under its original name.

Self-hosting also keeps the demo's promise of making no off-site requests, so the licence
and the architecture point the same way.

## Images and icons

- Prefer drawing what is needed as inline SVG owned by Stonemark. Every mark and glyph in
  the demo is Stonemark's own work, which is the cheapest licence there is.
- No stock photograph, icon set or illustration enters the repo without its licence
  recorded in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`, and no "found on the internet" image ever does.
- No screenshot of another vendor's product, and no carrier logo, in the demo or the
  marketing material.
- No AI-generated image where provenance and rights cannot be stated. Treat
  model-generated art as unclear title unless the tool's terms plainly grant commercial
  use.

## The archetype illustrations

The eight archetype SVGs are Stonemark's own work. They are no longer card backgrounds;
they remain available for archetype reference, empty states, report section marks and
client-deliverable cover art.

Screenshots of the demo may be used in Stonemark's own marketing. Keep the sample-data
