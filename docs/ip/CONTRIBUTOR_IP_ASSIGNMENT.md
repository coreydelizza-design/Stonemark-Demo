# Contributor IP assignment — short form

**Working draft to be replaced by counsel's version before anyone signs.** The IP plan
prices the full contractor stack at $1,500–4,000 with a lawyer; this file exists so that
briefing takes an hour instead of a day, and so nobody is ever given access on a
handshake while the paperwork is "coming".

Signed by every person, and every company acting through a person, **before** repository
access, tool access, or any briefing on the method. An assignment signed after the work
is a weaker instrument than one signed before it.

---

## 1. Parties and engagement

This assignment is between Stonemark Infrastructure Assurance LLC ("Stonemark") and
______________________ ("Contractor"), and covers all work performed for Stonemark,
whether under a Statement of Work or otherwise.

## 2. Assignment of work product

Contractor **hereby assigns** to Stonemark, on creation and without further act, all
right, title and interest worldwide in all work product created for Stonemark,
including code, designs, copy, datasets, documentation, and every improvement to
Stonemark's method, platform, scoring model or templates.

The present tense matters. "Agrees to assign" creates a promise to be enforced later;
"hereby assigns" transfers the right as the work is created. Independent contractor work
is owned by the contractor by default, so without this clause Stonemark does not own what
it paid for.

## 4. Moral rights and further assurance

To the extent moral rights cannot be assigned, Contractor waives their enforcement
against Stonemark. Contractor will sign any confirmatory document Stonemark reasonably
requests, including copyright registration papers, at Stonemark's cost.

## 5. Confidentiality

Contractor will keep confidential, indefinitely and after the engagement ends,
Stonemark's method, scoring model and caps, reconciliation rules, requirement packs,
playbooks, client list, pricing, and any client infrastructure data. Contractor will not
copy it outside Stonemark's controlled systems, and will return or destroy every copy on
request and on termination, confirming in writing that they have.

## 6. Third-party material and AI tools

Contractor will not incorporate third-party material into work product unless its licence
permits the use and the licence is recorded in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`. Contractor will
not place Stonemark's method, client data or credentials into any AI tool, and will
disclose which AI tools were used to produce work product.

## 7. No client data outside the platform

Contractor will not store client infrastructure data, carrier records or evidence
anywhere but Stonemark's controlled platform.

## 8. Independence

Contractor performs the work as an independent contractor, not an employee, is free to
work for others, provides their own equipment, and is paid per deliverable. Following
Stonemark's method, quality standards and deadlines does not change that.

**State-law warning, and the most important legal fact about this model:** California,
New Jersey, Massachusetts and other states apply an ABC test whose prong B requires the
work to fall outside the hiring company's usual business. A contractor delivering
assurance engagements for an assurance company fails prong B and would likely be an
employee in those states whatever this document says. Take state-specific advice, and
engage contractors through their own LLC or S-corp, before engaging anyone in an ABC
state.

## 9. Non-circumvention and non-solicitation

For [PENDING: term, with counsel] after the engagement, Contractor will not solicit or
accept assurance work directly from a Stonemark client they were introduced to through
Stonemark, and will not solicit Stonemark's contractors or clients away from Stonemark.
Stonemark is not seeking a non-compete: outright non-competes are unenforceable in many
states, and the protection here is confidentiality plus non-circumvention.
